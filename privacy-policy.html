Privacy Policy

How MTE collects, uses, and protects personal information.

Effective Date: 13 October 2026Last Updated: 13 October 2026

Welcome to MTE. We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, applications, educational services, courses, resources, and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account credentials
  • Educational information you voluntarily provide
  • Course/enrollment information
  • Information submitted through forms
  • Payment-related information necessary to process transactions

Technical Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referral information
  • Date and time of access
  • General usage and performance information

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Provide courses and educational services
  • Process registrations and enrollments
  • Process payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Improve our website and educational content
  • Monitor security and prevent misuse
  • Understand website usage and performance
  • Send educational updates or promotional communications where permitted

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences, analyze website traffic, improve functionality, and understand how users interact with our services.

Third-party services integrated into our website may also use cookies or similar technologies.

For more information, please see our Cookie Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers for:

  • Website hosting
  • Authentication
  • Cloud storage
  • Analytics
  • Email delivery
  • Payment processing
  • Security
  • Communication services

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Process payments
  • Work with service providers
  • Protect our users and systems
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with applicable laws or lawful requests

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, and complying with legal requirements.

8. Children's and Students' Privacy

Because MTE provides educational services, some users may be students or younger learners.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary for providing our services and do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

Where parental or guardian involvement is required, appropriate consent or supervision should be provided.

9. Your Choices

Subject to applicable law and our ability to provide the requested service, you may contact us regarding:

  • Access to your information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of information
  • Account-related requests
  • Marketing communication preferences
  • Privacy concerns

10. External Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies separately.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated Last Updated date.
