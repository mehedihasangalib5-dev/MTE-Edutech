1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They can help websites remember information, maintain sessions, improve functionality, and understand how visitors use the website.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We may use cookies for:

Essential Cookies

These may be required for:

  • Login
  • Authentication
  • Security
  • Account management
  • Basic website functionality

Preference Cookies

These may remember:

  • Language preferences
  • Interface settings
  • User preferences

Analytics Cookies

These may help us understand:

  • Which pages are visited
  • How visitors navigate the website
  • Website performance
  • General usage patterns

Marketing Cookies

If advertising or marketing services are implemented, cookies may be used to measure advertising performance or provide relevant advertising, subject to applicable requirements.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services integrated into our website may place cookies or similar technologies on your device.

Their use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Allow cookies from selected websites

Disabling essential cookies may cause some features of our website to stop working properly.

5. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this policy when our technology or services change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
