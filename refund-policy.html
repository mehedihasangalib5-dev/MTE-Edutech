Refund Policy

MTE’s policy on refunds.

Effective Date: 13 October 2026Last Updated: 13 October 2026

Thank you for choosing MTE.

We want users to understand our refund rules before purchasing any course, digital product, subscription, or service.

1. General Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the specific product or service purchased.

Where a product page provides a specific refund condition, that condition will apply to the relevant purchase.

2. Digital Products and Course Materials

Because digital educational materials may become accessible immediately after purchase, refunds may be limited once substantial access, downloading, or consumption has occurred.

However, we may consider refund requests in appropriate circumstances, including:

  • Duplicate payment
  • Technical problems preventing access
  • Payment completed but the purchased service was not provided
  • Accidental duplicate enrollment
  • Other circumstances approved by MTE

3. Course Refund Requests

Where a course includes a specific refund period, the applicable period will be clearly stated on the course purchase page.

Unless otherwise stated, a refund request should be submitted as soon as possible after identifying the problem.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may generally not be available when:

  • The user has substantially consumed the digital course/material
  • The user has downloaded protected materials
  • The user simply changes their mind after accessing the service
  • The user violates our Terms & Conditions
  • The issue results from the user's incompatible device, software, or internet connection
  • The user provides false or misleading information

These conditions are subject to applicable consumer-protection rights.

5. Duplicate Payments

If you were charged more than once for the same purchase due to a technical or payment-processing error, please contact us with the relevant transaction information.

After verification, the duplicate amount may be refunded.

6. How to Request a Refund

Send a refund request to:

Email: mte.educationtech@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Name
  • Registered email address
  • Order/transaction ID
  • Product/course name
  • Date of purchase
  • Reason for the request

We may request additional information to verify the transaction.

7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will generally be processed through the original payment method where technically possible.

The time required for the amount to appear in your account may depend on the relevant payment provider or financial institution.

8. Fraudulent Transactions

If a transaction appears unauthorized or fraudulent, contact us immediately.

We may investigate suspicious transactions and may suspend related accounts where reasonably necessary to protect users and our services.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time.

The policy applicable to a purchase will generally be determined according to the terms presented at the time of purchase, subject to applicable law.
