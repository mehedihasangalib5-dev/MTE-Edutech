Who We Are

MTE is a technology-driven education company dedicated to helping people learn, develop practical skills, and adapt to an increasingly digital world.

Work and daily life are moving online faster than most learning paths can keep up. Good knowledge exists, but it is often scattered, too complex, or disconnected from how it is actually used. MTE exists to close that gap.

We are building for students, early-career professionals, and curious people of any background who want skills they can apply. Technology and education belong together: technology changes what people need to learn, and education decides who gets to benefit from it.

What makes us different

Practical first
We start from what people can apply, then build understanding around it.
Plain language
Clear explanations over jargon, so more people can get started.
Built for the long term
We are growing deliberately, with a long-term view instead of short-term claims.

Our Story

Every meaningful journey begins with an idea.

MTE started with a simple belief — that quality knowledge and technology should be easier to understand, access, and apply.

Founded in 2026, the company is being built around education, technology, digital skills, and innovation, with a long-term vision of creating meaningful value for the next generation.

  1. 2026

    The Beginning

    MTE is founded in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

  2. Future

    Growing the Vision

    Building programs, products, and partnerships as the company grows.

  3. Long Term

    Creating Meaningful Impact

    Becoming a trusted name in technology and education.
